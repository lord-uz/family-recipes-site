Salmon chowder

Status: Previously tried by the family; specific adjustments not yet recorded.
Source: Allrecipes, Salmon Chowder (photographed printout).
Serves: 8. Prep: 15 minutes. Cook: 30 minutes. Total: 45 minutes.

Ingredients

  • 3 tbsp butter
  • ¾ cup chopped onion
  • ½ cup chopped celery
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 2 cups diced potatoes
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 2 cups (about 480 mL) chicken stock
  • 1 tsp salt, or to taste
  • 1 tsp ground black pepper
  • 1 tsp dried dill
  • 2 × 16 oz cans salmon (approximately 2 × 454 g), drained if necessary and flaked
  • 1 × 12 US fl oz can evaporated milk (approximately 355 mL)
  • 1 × 15 oz can creamed corn (approximately 425 g)
  • ½ lb (approximately 225 g) grated cheddar cheese

Method

  1. Melt butter in a large saucepan over medium heat. Sauté onion and celery with garlic powder until the onion is softened.
  2. Add potatoes, carrots, chicken stock, salt, pepper and dried dill. Bring to the boil, reduce the heat, cover and simmer for about 20 minutes, until the vegetables are tender.
  3. Stir in canned salmon, evaporated milk, creamed corn and grated cheddar.
  4. Heat gently, stirring until the cheese melts and everything is hot. Avoid vigorous boiling after adding the milk and cheese.
  5. Taste and adjust seasoning before serving.

Notes

  • The original recipe uses canned salmon, not fresh salmon.
  • The salmon, evaporated milk, creamed corn and cheddar are added at the end; they give the chowder its rich, creamy texture.
  • The original salt amount is 1 tsp, but canned salmon, stock and cheddar can also contribute salt.
  • US package sizes have been translated approximately to metric; use the closest available Australian package sizes.
  • This is a separate recipe from the family's seafood chowder.
