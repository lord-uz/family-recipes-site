Sticky raisin and apple muffins

Status: Previously tried by the family; any adaptations not yet recorded.
Source: Sticky Raisin and Apple Muffins, photographed cookbook page (book title not visible).
Makes: 12 large muffins. Bake: 15–20 minutes at 200°C.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups wholemeal flour
  • 2 tsp mixed spice
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • ½ cup brown sugar
  • 1 cup grated apple, skin on
  • ½ cup sticky raisins
  • 1 cup non-fat milk
  • 2 tbsp polyunsaturated oil (or another neutral vegetable oil)
  • 1 egg

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 200°C conventional. Grease a 12-hole large muffin tin.
  2. Combine wholemeal flour, mixed spice, baking powder, brown sugar, grated apple and raisins in a large mixing bowl.
  3. In a second bowl, whisk milk, oil and egg together.
  4. Add the wet mixture to the dry ingredients and fold gently until just incorporated. Avoid overmixing.
  5. Fill prepared muffin holes about three-quarters full.
  6. Bake 15–20 minutes, until golden brown and cooked through. Let cool briefly before removing from the tin.

Notes

  • The original calls for grated apple with the skin left on.
  • Approximate nutrition from the printed recipe: 150 calories (629 kJ) per muffin.
  • The printed ingredient uses the term “sticky raisins”; ordinary moist raisins should work.
  • Wholemeal flour is specified in the original; a flour substitution may require adjusting liquid.
