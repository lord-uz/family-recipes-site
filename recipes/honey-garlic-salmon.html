Honey garlic salmon

Status: Previously tried by the family; specific adjustments not yet recorded.
Source: Nagi, RecipeTin Eats, Honey Garlic Salmon (5 Ingredients, 15 Minutes) (photographed printout).
Serves: 2. Prep: 5 minutes. Cook: 10 minutes. Total: 15 minutes.

Ingredients

Sauce

  • 4 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce (regular or light)
  • 1 tbsp white wine vinegar (or another vinegar, except balsamic)
  • 1 large garlic clove (or 2 small), minced

Salmon

  • 2 skinless salmon or trout fillets, about 200 g each
  • Olive oil, for frying
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

Optional garnish

  • Sesame seeds
  • Finely sliced chives or spring onions

Method

  1. Pat the salmon dry and season lightly with salt and pepper.
  2. Mix honey, soy sauce, vinegar and minced garlic in a small bowl.
  3. Heat a little olive oil in a non-stick frying pan over medium-high heat (slightly lower if the stove runs hot). Cook salmon on the first side for 3–4 minutes until golden; turn and cook the other side for 2–3 minutes.
  4. Pour in the sauce. Cook for about 1 minute, spooning it over the salmon, until it thickens slightly. If it reduces too much before the salmon is cooked as desired, add water 1 tbsp at a time.
  5. Transfer salmon to serving plates, spoon over the sauce, and add sesame seeds or chives/spring onions if desired.

Notes

  • Doneness: The original author prefers a coral-coloured, medium-rare centre, about 50°C, or 55°C for medium, measured in the thickest part. Residual heat continues cooking the fish after removal. Cook more thoroughly if preferred or for anyone who should avoid undercooked fish.
  • Sauce balance: Honey provides sweetness, soy sauce saltiness, and vinegar acidity. Taste and adjust on a subsequent trial if the family prefers less sweetness or soy sauce.
  • Timing: Thickness of fillets and pan temperature affect cooking time.
  • Serving: The recipe's estimated nutrition assumes all sauce is consumed, though some may remain in the pan.
