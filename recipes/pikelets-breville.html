Pikelets

Status: Family-tested and works well. Source: Breville BCP200 manual, p. 16.

Ingredients

  • 150 g self-raising wheat flour (1 cup)
  • Pinch of salt
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 egg (approximately 60 g), beaten
  • 190 ml milk (¾ cup)
  • 20 g melted butter (1 tbsp)

Method

  1. Sift together self-raising flour, salt and sugar.
  2. Beat egg, milk and butter together, then stir into the dry mix until smooth. Let the mixture stand for 5 minutes.
  3. Heat the Breville plate at setting 1–2. Spoon on about 1 tbsp per pikelet and spread to 5–6 cm; approximately six fit at once.
  4. Once bubbles appear and pop (usually after 1–2 minutes), turn carefully with a non-metal spatula.
  5. Cook the other side until lightly golden and serve warm.

Family / coeliac notes

  • This is the tested wheat-flour recipe, not a gluten-free formulation.
  • A separate gluten-free adaptation and dedicated/clean cooking equipment are needed for the coeliac family member; that adaptation remains untested.
