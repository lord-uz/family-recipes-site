Easy beef stir-fry with mixed noodles

Status: Tested and liked by everyone. Low-effort weekday option.
Serves: Two adults, one teenager and one five-year-old (approximately 3.5 adult portions).

Ingredients — quantities actually used

  • 500 g lean beef stir-fry strips
  • Approximately 700 g Birds Eye frozen Chinese-style stir-fry vegetables
  • 3 nests thin wheat-based egg noodles (for family members who eat gluten)
  • 1 portion rice noodles (for the family member with coeliac disease)
  • Passage to Asia Hoisin & Garlic stir-fry sauce — 1 full 200 g pouch
  • Small amount of cooking oil, as needed

Method (adapted to a modest-powered burner)

  1. Cook egg noodles and rice noodles separately, using separate water, pans, strainers and utensils. Set aside.
  2. Heat the wok well. Fry the beef in small batches rather than overcrowding it. Set browned batches aside.
  3. Stir-fry the frozen vegetables in batches, allowing moisture to evaporate. Do not overload the wok; a weak burner struggles to maintain heat, especially with frozen vegetables.
  4. Return beef and vegetables to the wok in manageable portions. Add the hoisin and garlic stir-fry sauce and cook until the beef is thoroughly cooked and everything is hot.
  5. Serve beef and vegetables with rice noodles on the gluten-free plate and egg noodles on the other plates, using separate serving utensils.

Coeliac precautions

  • Passage to Asia Hoisin & Garlic sauce is currently marketed in Australia as gluten-free (check the packet every time in case the formulation changes). It contains soy and sesame.
  • Verify the exact frozen-vegetable product and rice-noodle labels, and avoid cross-contact from wheat egg noodles.
  • Prepare and plate the gluten-free portion first with clean utensils, before handling the egg noodles. Do not combine wheat noodles with the common wok mixture.

Family feedback and adjustments

  • Everyone liked it.
  • Low effort: a useful repeat weekday dinner.
  • The existing wok is adequate, but the burner has limited heat output. Cook both meat and frozen vegetables in small batches.
  • Used the entire 200 g pouch of sauce.
  • The meal fed the family of four (approximately 3.5 adult-equivalent portions) with about one extra individual portion left for the next day.
  • Not the same as the previously proposed chicken stir-fry with tamari; retain both recipes separately.
