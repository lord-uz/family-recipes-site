Green split pea and bacon soup

Status: Previously tried by the family; personal adjustments not yet recorded.
Source: Food.com, Green Split Pea and Bacon Soup, submitted by I'mPat; based on a Super Food Ideas July 2008 recipe (photographed printout).
Serves: 4. Total time: About 55 minutes, depending on how quickly the peas soften.

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 large brown onion, finely chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed or minced
  • 1¼ cups dried green split peas, rinsed
  • 2 cups (500 mL) chicken stock (homemade ham stock is an option mentioned by the author)
  • 2 cups (500 mL) cold water
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • 125 g bacon rashers, rind removed
  • ½ tsp paprika, to serve
  • 4 crusty bread rolls, optional, to serve

Method

  1. Heat olive oil in a saucepan over medium-high heat. Cook onion and garlic, stirring, for approximately 3 minutes, until the onion softens.
  2. Add rinsed split peas and stir to coat.
  3. Pour in chicken stock and water; bring to the boil.
  4. Reduce heat and simmer partially covered for 25–45 minutes, until the peas are tender. Check and stir as needed.
  5. Remove from heat and partially blend the soup, using a stick blender in the pot or blending approximately half of it separately, to achieve the preferred balance of smoothness and texture.
  6. Add lemon juice and warm for about 3 minutes.
  7. Meanwhile, fry bacon over medium-high heat until crisp. Drain on paper towel and crumble or chop into small pieces.
  8. Ladle soup into bowls, top with bacon and paprika, and serve with a bread roll if desired.

Notes

  • Cooking time: The source reports that dried split peas can take 25–45 minutes to become tender, so judge by texture rather than the clock.
  • Texture: Blending some rather than all of the soup produces a thick, partly textured result.
  • Bacon is a topping: Cooking it separately keeps it crisp at serving time.
  • Stock: The original author mentions using homemade ham stock instead of chicken stock.
