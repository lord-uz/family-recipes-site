Grilled beef kofta with cucumber-yoghurt sauce and rice

Status: Proposed — new BBQ dinner.
Serves: Two adults, one teenager and one younger child, with possible leftovers.
Time: About 35–45 minutes including shaping and rice cooking.
Equipment: Napoleon TravelQ or another BBQ/grill; frying pan or oven grill as a fallback.

Ingredients

Kofta

  • 1 kg beef mince (ideally not ultra-lean, approximately 15–20% fat for juiciness)
  • 1 small onion, finely grated, with excess moisture squeezed out
  • 3 garlic cloves, finely minced
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 tsp ground coriander
  • 1 tsp sweet paprika
  • ½ tsp ground cinnamon (optional)
  • 1½ tsp fine salt, or to taste
  • Black pepper
  • 2 tbsp chopped flat-leaf parsley (optional)
  • A little oil for the grill

Cucumber-yoghurt sauce and serving

  • 300 g plain Greek yoghurt
  • 1 cucumber, coarsely grated or diced (squeeze excess moisture if grated)
  • ½ lemon, juiced
  • 1 small garlic clove, finely grated (optional)
  • Salt, pepper and optional fresh mint or dill
  • 250–300 g uncooked rice
  • 2 tomatoes and optional extra cucumber or leafy salad
  • Lemon wedges to serve

Method

  1. Start the rice according to its packet directions. Mix yoghurt, cucumber, lemon, optional garlic and herbs; refrigerate while preparing the kofta.
  2. Combine mince, squeezed grated onion, garlic, spices, salt, pepper and parsley in a bowl. Mix with your hands until evenly blended and slightly tacky. Do not add breadcrumbs or egg.
  3. Divide into 10–12 portions and shape into short, fairly thick sausage-shaped kofta. Press firmly so they hold together. Refrigerate for 10–15 minutes if time permits; short wooden or metal skewers are optional, not essential.
  4. Preheat the BBQ to medium-high and lightly oil the grill. Cook kofta for approximately 8–12 minutes, turning every few minutes for even browning, until safely cooked through (aim for 75°C in the centre for minced meat). Cooking time depends on their thickness and the grill.
  5. Rest briefly and serve with fluffy rice, yoghurt sauce, tomato salad and lemon.

Family notes

  • Designed for a quick grilled dinner rather than another stir-fry, using the existing balcony BBQ.
  • With 1 kg mince, expect generous portions and potentially some leftovers; alternatively use 750–800 g mince and reduce seasoning proportionately.
  • Avoid making the kofta too thin; thicker kofta are easier to keep juicy while grilling.
  • If the weather is poor, sear in a frying pan or cook under an oven grill, turning to brown both sides.
