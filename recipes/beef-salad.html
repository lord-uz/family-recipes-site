Beef salad with peppercorn or coriander dressing

Status: Previously tried by the family; specific adjustments not yet recorded.
Source: Beef Salad, photographed cookbook page (full attribution not visible).
Serves: Approximately 3–4, depending on the amount of salad vegetables and accompaniments.

Ingredients

  • 400 g lean beef (fillet, sirloin or rump), rare-roasted, grilled, pan-grilled or barbecued, then cooled
  • 4–5 lettuce leaves, washed and roughly torn
  • 4 cups bite-sized salad vegetables, such as capsicum (red/yellow/green), celery, red onion or spring onions, cucumber, radish, cherry or quartered tomatoes, avocado, mushrooms or sprouts; alternatively, lightly blanched spring vegetables

Dressing

  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tbsp red wine vinegar
  • 1 garlic clove, finely chopped
  • Freshly ground black pepper, to taste
  • Either: 2 tbsp drained green peppercorns or 2 tsp minced fresh or prepared coriander leaves
  • 2 tbsp finely chopped spring onion

Method

  1. Cook the beef by roasting, grilling, pan-grilling or barbecuing, to your preferred doneness. Allow to cool, then slice thinly across the grain.
  2. Arrange torn lettuce on a serving platter or individual plates. Layer on the sliced beef and prepared salad vegetables.
  3. Whisk olive oil, red wine vinegar, garlic, black pepper, green peppercorns or coriander, and finely chopped spring onion.
  4. Spoon the dressing over the beef and salad, just enough to moisten. Serve straight away.

Notes

  • The original recipe calls for cold, cooked beef, sliced thinly. It works well for using leftover roast beef or steak.
  • The green peppercorn and coriander options are alternatives, with different quantities, rather than both being required.
  • Vegetables are flexible; use a mixture of raw salad vegetables or briefly blanched spring vegetables.
  • The visible source's final garnish suggestion is cut off in the photograph; an optional leafy herb garnish is fine.
