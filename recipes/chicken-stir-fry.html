Chicken stir-fry with rice noodles

Status: Proposed; not yet cooked by the family.
Serves: Two adults, one teenager and one five-year-old (about 3.5 adult portions).
Time: About 25–30 minutes.

Ingredients

  • 650–750 g boneless chicken thighs or breast, sliced into thin strips
  • 750 g frozen stir-fry vegetable mix
  • 300–350 g dry rice noodles, verified gluten-free
  • 3 tbsp gluten-free tamari (adjust to taste)
  • 2 cloves garlic, finely chopped
  • 1–2 tbsp neutral cooking oil
  • Optional: 1 tsp grated ginger
  • Optional: 1 tsp honey or sugar, if a slightly sweeter sauce is preferred
  • Optional: lemon or lime to serve

Method

  1. Prepare the rice noodles according to the packet directions. Drain and set aside.
  2. Heat a large wok or frying pan with some oil. Stir-fry the sliced chicken in batches so it browns rather than steams. Cook thoroughly; chicken must reach at least 75°C in the thickest part.
  3. Return all the chicken to the pan. Add the frozen vegetables and stir-fry until fully heated and tender, allowing excess water to evaporate.
  4. Stir in garlic, optional ginger, gluten-free tamari and optional honey. Cook for another minute or two.
  5. Toss in the cooked rice noodles, or serve chicken and vegetables over the noodles. Add lemon or lime if wanted.

Coeliac notes

Use rice noodles labelled gluten-free and verified gluten-free tamari; ordinary soy sauce generally contains wheat. Check the frozen vegetable mix and any added seasonings. Use clean utensils, colanders and pans to avoid cross-contact. If anyone wants ordinary wheat-based egg noodles, cook and serve them separately from the coeliac-safe meal.

Planning notes

  • Designed as an easy Monday dinner requiring no Sunday meal preparation.
  • Chicken can be replaced by thinly sliced beef stir-fry strips; adjust cooking time accordingly.
  • Frozen vegetables can be bought ahead and stored in the freezer.
  • Best eaten fresh, particularly the noodles.
