Slow-cooked beef and vegetable casserole

Status: Proposed. Yield: Roughly 7 adult-equivalent portions. Active preparation: Approximately 60 minutes when cooking solo at a comfortable pace.

Ingredients

  • 1.4–1.6 kg diced chuck beef
  • 2 onions, sliced or diced
  • 4 carrots, chopped
  • 800 g–1 kg potatoes, cut into large pieces
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • 500–700 ml gluten-free beef stock
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste (optional, check ingredients)
  • 2 bay leaves; optional thyme
  • Salt and pepper

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 155–160°C. Pat beef dry and brown in batches in a heavy, oven-safe casserole dish or in a frying pan.
  2. Soften onions and carrots for a few minutes, then add garlic and optional tomato paste.
  3. Combine beef, vegetables, stock and herbs in a covered roasting or casserole dish. Liquid should partly surround the beef, not necessarily submerge it.
  4. Cover tightly and bake 2½–3 hours, checking occasionally, until beef is tender.
  5. Add potatoes during the final 45–60 minutes; add a little more stock or water if required.
  6. Adjust seasoning and serve. For a second dinner, refrigerate promptly and reheat thoroughly, perhaps with buckwheat instead of potatoes.

Family notes

  • Planned as a longer Sunday cook with leftovers for the following week.
  • Do not add wheat flour to thicken. If needed, simmer the sauce uncovered or thicken with a small amount of verified gluten-free cornflour slurry.
  • Allow approximately 60 minutes of active preparation when cooking solo without rushing. Browning meat in batches and preparing vegetables are best done sequentially; with two people or more effective browning equipment, this may be faster.
  • Not yet family-tested.
