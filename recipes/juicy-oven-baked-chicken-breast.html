Juicy oven-baked chicken breast

Status: Previously tried by the family; specific adjustments not yet recorded.
Source: Juicy Oven Baked Chicken Breast, by Karina (photographed recipe).
Serves: 4. Prep: 10 minutes. Cook: about 16–18 minutes, plus resting.

Ingredients

  • 4 large chicken breasts, approximately 200 g each (800 g total)
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp packed brown sugar
  • 1½ tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp dried oregano (or thyme, parsley or rosemary)
  • 1 tsp salt (or seasoned salt)
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • ½ tsp onion powder
  • ¼ tsp cracked black pepper
  • 1 tsp chilli powder (or ½ tsp cayenne), optional

Optional garlic-butter finish

  • 3 tbsp butter
  • 2 garlic cloves, finely chopped
  • 2 tsp freshly chopped parsley

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 220°C conventional or 200°C fan-forced.
  2. Pound chicken breasts to an even thickness of approximately 2 cm to promote even cooking.
  3. Combine brown sugar, paprika, dried herbs, salt, garlic and onion powders, pepper and optional chilli.
  4. Line a baking tray with foil or baking paper. Add chicken, sprinkle with the spice mixture, drizzle with olive oil and rub to coat all sides.
  5. Bake for 16–18 minutes, depending on thickness, until golden at the edges and the thickest part reaches 75°C. Check with a meat thermometer rather than relying only on time.
  6. For extra colour, remove baking paper before grilling; grill/broil on high for the final 2–3 minutes, monitoring closely to avoid burning.
  7. Rest the chicken for 5 minutes before serving.
  8. Optional: While chicken bakes, melt butter in a small pan, briefly sauté garlic (about 30 seconds), remove from heat and stir in parsley. Mix with collected chicken juices and spoon over chicken.

Notes

  • Even thickness and avoiding overcooking are the main ways to keep chicken breast juicy.
  • Brown sugar helps the spice coating colour and caramelise; omit or reduce it if a less sweet result is preferred, noting browning may change.
  • The printed recipe lists 26 minutes total, although resting and optional grilling may add a few minutes.
