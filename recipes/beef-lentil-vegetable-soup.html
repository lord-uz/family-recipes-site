Beef and lentil soup with vegetables

Status: Previously cooked by the family (provided as a photographed recipe).
Source: Nagi, RecipeTin Eats, Beef & Lentil Soup with Vegetables. Adapted into concise kitchen notes from the printed copy.
Serves: 6. Time: About 40 minutes according to the original (allow extra preparation time if needed).

Ingredients

Moroccan-style spice mixture

  • 2½ tbsp Moroccan spice mix (store-bought)
  • 1 tsp ground allspice (or mixed spice)
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 tsp paprika
  • ¾ tsp black pepper
  • ½ tsp salt, then adjust to taste

Soup

  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 garlic cloves, finely chopped
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 500 g lean beef mince
  • 1¼ cups dried green or brown lentils (or 2 × 400 g cans, drained)
  • 2 carrots, diced about 1 cm
  • 2 celery stalks, diced about 1 cm
  • 2 small zucchini (or 1 large), diced about 1 cm
  • 100 g green beans, cut into roughly 1.5 cm pieces
  • 800 g crushed canned tomatoes
  • 1 litre low-sodium beef stock
  • 3 cups water

To serve (optional)

  • Plain or Greek yoghurt
  • Coriander leaves or sliced spring onions

Method

  1. Combine the spice ingredients in a small bowl.
  2. Heat oil in a large pot over medium-high heat. Cook onion and garlic for about 3 minutes, until softened and lightly golden.
  3. Add beef mince, breaking it up as it browns. Stir in 2 tbsp of the combined spice mixture; cook another 2 minutes.
  4. Stir in carrot and celery for about 1 minute.
  5. Add tomatoes, stock, water, dried lentils and the remaining spice mixture. Bring to a simmer, then reduce heat to a gentle simmer.
  6. After 15 minutes, add zucchini and green beans.
  7. Cook for another 10–15 minutes, until lentils are soft and vegetables are tender. Season to taste.
  8. Serve with yoghurt and coriander or spring onions if desired.

Useful variations and notes

  • Canned lentils: Use 2 × 400 g cans, drained, instead of dried; add around 15 minutes into simmering, for the final 10–15 minutes.
  • Vegetables: Substitute roughly 6–8 cups of vegetables, adding firm vegetables earlier and delicate vegetables later. Spinach or kale can go in near the end.
  • Spicing: The original suggests Mexican/taco, Indian curry with garam masala, Greek oregano/fennel with lemon, Italian herbs/parmesan, Cajun or shawarma-style alternatives. These replace or adjust the Moroccan-style spice mixture as appropriate.
  • Stock: Prefer low-sodium stock because bought spice blends may contain salt. When using stock cubes instead, a longer gentle sauté of onion, carrot and celery helps develop flavour.
  • Lentils: Dried green or brown lentils are preferred; the original advises against French/Puy lentils for this texture because they retain firmness.
  • Storage (original guidance): Refrigerate up to 5 days or freeze up to 3 months, with appropriate cooling and storage.
