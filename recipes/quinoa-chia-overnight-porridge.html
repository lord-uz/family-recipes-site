Quick gluten-free quinoa flake and chia overnight porridge

Status: Made several times.
Yield: Six 35 g servings of dry mix, ready for overnight soaking.
Method: Overnight, no cooking required. Active preparation takes only a few minutes.

Dry mix — 6 portions (210 g total)

  • 105 g chia seeds
  • 65 g quinoa flakes
  • 25 g dried cranberries
  • 15 g desiccated coconut

Mix all dry ingredients thoroughly and store in an airtight container until needed.

Per serving

  • 35 g dry mix
  • 150–170 mL milk of choice

Method

  1. Portion 35 g dry mix into a bowl or jar.
  2. Add 150–170 mL milk and stir thoroughly so the chia seeds are evenly dispersed.
  3. Cover and refrigerate overnight.
  4. Stir again in the morning. Add a splash of milk if a thinner consistency is preferred, then serve cold or gently warmed if desired.

Notes

  • Texture: Quinoa flakes soften quickly, making this smoother and less chewy than many oat-based breakfast mixtures. The relatively high chia content creates a thick consistency.
  • Optional variation from the original conversation: Replace half the quinoa flakes with rice flakes if you want a little more texture.
  • Ingredient examples originally discussed: Macro Organic Quinoa Flakes, Macro Black Chia Seeds, fine desiccated coconut and Ocean Spray Craisins Original.
  • Gluten-free suitability: Select appropriate quinoa/rice flakes and other ingredients for the household.
  • This is the overnight recipe from September 2026, not a separately developed cooked quinoa porridge.
  • This recipe is aimed to provide gluten-free alternative to overnight oats with chia.
