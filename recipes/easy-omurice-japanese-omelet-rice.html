Easy omurice (Japanese omelet rice)

Status: Tested by the family; uses substantially less soy sauce than the original, added to taste.
Source: Tiffany, Easy Omurice Recipe (Japanese Omelet Rice) (photographed printout).
Serves: 2. Prep: 5 minutes. Cook: 15 minutes. Total: 20 minutes.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked white rice (quick-cooking rice is fine)
  • ½ cup cooked diced or shredded chicken
  • 2 tbsp toasted sesame oil, divided
  • ¼ white or yellow onion, diced
  • Soy sauce, to taste (family uses much less than the original 60 mL)
  • 1 tbsp ketchup, plus extra to serve
  • ½ cup defrosted frozen mixed vegetables (peas, carrots and corn)
  • 4 large eggs
  • Salt and pepper, to taste
  • ¼ cup grated cheese (e.g. cheddar or Monterey Jack)
  • 1 tbsp olive oil

Method

  1. Whisk the eggs together with olive oil; set aside.
  2. In a medium frying pan over medium-high heat, cook diced onion in 1 tbsp sesame oil for 2–3 minutes until translucent.
  3. Add cooked rice, the remaining 1 tbsp sesame oil, ketchup and a small amount of soy sauce. Stir-fry for 3–4 minutes, then taste and add more soy sauce only if needed.
  4. Add defrosted vegetables and cooked chicken; heat through. Transfer the rice filling to a bowl and cover to keep warm.
  5. Reduce heat to medium. Pour half the egg mixture into the pan, tilting quickly to form an even layer. Sprinkle over half the cheese.
  6. Cook for 1–2 minutes until the egg is mostly set, with its upper surface still slightly soft.
  7. Place half the rice filling in the middle of the egg. Fold the egg over the filling with a spatula. Carefully slide or turn onto a plate; the source suggests using a paper towel to gently shape the omelet and tuck in the edges.
  8. Repeat with the remaining egg, cheese and rice for a second omurice. Serve warm with additional ketchup, if desired.

Notes

  • Optional spicy version: Add 1–2 tsp sriracha to the rice filling.
  • Family adjustment — soy sauce: Add to taste rather than following the original ¼ cup (60 mL) for two servings. The family prefers considerably less.
  • Rice: Use properly cooled and refrigerated cooked rice if prepared ahead; reheat until thoroughly hot.
  • The original recipe uses a cheese-filled egg wrapper and soy-sesame flavoured rice, rather than a strictly traditional omurice filling.
