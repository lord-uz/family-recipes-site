Pancakes

Status: Family-tested and works well. Source: Breville BCP200 manual, p. 16.

Ingredients

  • 150 g self-raising wheat flour (1 cup)
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tbsp caster sugar
  • 1 egg (approximately 60 g), beaten
  • 250 ml milk (1 cup)
  • ½ tsp vanilla essence
  • 30 g melted butter (1½ tbsp)

Method

  1. Combine sifted flour, baking powder and sugar.
  2. Whisk egg, milk, vanilla and butter together, then mix into the dry ingredients until smooth. Rest for 5 minutes.
  3. Preheat the Breville crêpe plate to setting 1–2.
  4. For each pancake, use 2–3 tbsp batter to make a round about 10–12 cm across.
  5. Cook about 1–2 minutes until the surface is no longer wet; turn with a non-metal spatula and cook until lightly golden.
  6. Repeat and serve, for example with fruit, lemon or maple syrup.

Family / coeliac notes

  • This records the tested standard wheat-flour version; a gluten-free flour swap is not yet tested.
  • Prepare gluten-free portions separately with verified ingredients and no shared contaminated utensils or cooking surface.
