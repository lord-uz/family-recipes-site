Garlic herb butter roast chicken

Status: Previously tried by the family; specific adjustments not yet recorded.
Source: Garlic Herb Butter Roast Chicken (photographed printout; author not identified).
Main cooking time: About 1 hour 15–20 minutes for a 2 kg bird, plus 10 minutes resting (check temperature, not just time).

Ingredients

  • 1 whole chicken, approximately 2 kg; giblets and neck removed
  • ¼ cup unsalted butter (about 60 g), melted
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • ¼ cup (60 mL) dry white wine, optional (e.g. Sauvignon Blanc or Chardonnay)
  • 1 lemon, halved
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste
  • 2 tbsp chopped fresh parsley
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 whole head garlic, cut in half crosswise
  • 3 fresh rosemary sprigs

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 220°C conventional or 200°C fan-forced. Line a roasting tray with foil or lightly grease it.
  2. Remove any excess fat and remaining feathers; pat the chicken dry with paper towels.
  3. Pour olive oil, melted butter, optional wine and juice from half the lemon over the chicken, including under the skin and inside the cavity. Season the exterior and cavity generously with salt and pepper; scatter over parsley.
  4. Rub minced garlic over and under the chicken skin, spreading the seasonings evenly.
  5. Put the halved garlic head, rosemary sprigs and squeezed lemon half inside the cavity. Tie the legs together with kitchen string.
  6. Put chicken breast-side up in the tray and roast for approximately 1 hour 15–20 minutes, basting about halfway through. Check doneness with a thermometer in the thickest part of the thigh, avoiding the bone: aim for at least 75°C.
  7. Baste again; optionally grill/broil for 2–3 minutes to brown the skin, watching carefully.
  8. Remove from oven, loosely cover with foil and rest 10 minutes. Serve with pan juices and wedges cut from the remaining lemon half.

Notes

  • Larger chicken (source guidance): Roast at the initial high temperature for about 1 hour, then lower to 160°C, cover and continue roasting, basting twice, for approximately another 45–60 minutes or until cooked through. Total time depends on size.
  • Wine: Optional; the recipe also works without it.
  • Food handling: Keep raw chicken refrigerated until preparation rather than deliberately leaving it at room temperature for an extended period.
  • Check doneness: The original also mentions clear juices, but internal temperature is more reliable.
