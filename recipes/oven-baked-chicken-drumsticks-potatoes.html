Oven-baked chicken drumsticks with potatoes

Status: Previously tried by the family; any personal modifications have not yet been recorded.
Source: EatingWell, Oven-Baked Chicken Drumsticks with Potatoes (photographed printout).
Serves: 4. Active: 25 minutes. Total: About 45 minutes.

Ingredients

  • 2 medium sweet potatoes, cut into about 2.5 cm pieces
  • 1 medium russet potato (or suitable roasting potato), cut into about 2.5 cm pieces
  • 1 medium yellow onion, chopped
  • 1 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
  • 2 tsp chopped fresh rosemary
  • 2 tsp chopped fresh thyme
  • ¾ tsp ground black pepper, divided
  • ½ tsp salt plus a pinch, divided
  • 2 lb (about 900 g) chicken drumsticks
  • 3 tsp dried herb seasoning blend, divided (e.g. garlic-and-herb or Cajun)
  • 6 tbsp sour cream
  • 2 tsp lemon juice
  • Cooking spray or a little extra oil for the tray

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 230°C (450°F). Lightly grease a rimmed baking tray.
  2. In a large bowl toss sweet potatoes, potato and onion with olive oil, rosemary, thyme, ½ tsp pepper and ¼ tsp salt. Spread in an even layer over the tray.
  3. In the same bowl coat drumsticks with 2 tsp dried herb seasoning, the remaining ¼ tsp pepper and ¼ tsp salt. Arrange chicken over the vegetables.
  4. Roast about 30 minutes, stirring the vegetables once, until potatoes are tender and chicken reaches at least 74°C (165°F) at the thickest part, without touching the bone.
  5. Move tray to the upper oven position and use the grill/broiler on high for about 3 minutes, watching closely, to brown the chicken.
  6. Stir together sour cream, lemon juice, remaining 1 tsp dried herb seasoning and a pinch of salt. Serve as a sauce with the chicken and vegetables.

Notes

  • Use a salt-free herb seasoning blend if available to manage the overall salt level.
  • If using fan-forced heat, a slightly lower initial oven setting may be appropriate; judge by browning and internal chicken temperature.
  • The source uses the oven's bottom rack for roasting, then top rack for broiling/grilling.
  • Source's suggested seasoning variations include garlic-and-herb and Cajun.
