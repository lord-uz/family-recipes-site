Curried corn and rice soup

Status: Previously tried by the family; adjustments not yet recorded.
Source: Curried Corn and Rice Soup, photographed cookbook page 14 (full attribution not visible).
Serves: 6.

Ingredients

  • 2 rashers lean bacon, rind and visible fat removed, finely chopped
  • 1 tbsp polyunsaturated oil (or another neutral cooking oil)
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 1½ tsp curry powder
  • 1 tbsp flour
  • 1 cup (250 mL) low-fat milk
  • 3 cups (750 mL) chicken stock
  • ½ cup uncooked rice
  • 1 carrot, finely chopped
  • 1 × 440 g can whole-kernel corn (no added salt), drained
  • 2 tbsp finely chopped fresh parsley
  • Freshly ground black pepper, to taste

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a heavy-based saucepan. Sauté bacon, onion and curry powder together for 2–3 minutes.
  2. Stir in the flour. Gradually add milk, stirring continuously until the mixture thickens into a curry-flavoured white sauce. Remove from heat and set aside.
  3. In a separate large saucepan, bring chicken stock to a boil. Add the uncooked rice, cover and simmer for 15–20 minutes, or until tender.
  4. Add the chopped carrot and drained corn to the stock and rice; simmer for about 10 minutes more, until carrot softens.
  5. Stir the reserved curry sauce into the soup, then add parsley and freshly ground black pepper.
  6. Reheat gently until hot, without boiling, and serve.

Notes

  • The original instruction “add the curry sauce” refers to the bacon, onion, curry powder, flour and milk mixture made in steps 1–2; it is not a separate ingredient.
  • The original recipe suggests wholegrain bread and fresh fruit as accompaniments.
  • Listed nutrition on the printed page: 648 kJ (154 kcal) per serving.
  • The thickening comes from flour and milk, while rice and corn make the soup filling.
