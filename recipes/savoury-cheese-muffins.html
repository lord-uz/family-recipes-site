Savoury cheese muffins

Status: Previously tried by the family; any individual adjustments have not yet been recorded.
Source: Nagi, RecipeTin Eats, Savoury Cheese Muffins (photographed printout).
Makes: 11 standard muffins. Prep: 15 minutes. Bake: 22–25 minutes (the printed recipe header says 20 minutes).

Ingredients

Garlic butter and cheese

  • 50 g salted butter (3 tbsp)
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 200 g grated cheddar cheese (about 2 cups)

Dry ingredients

  • 300 g plain flour (about 2 cups)
  • 1½ tsp baking powder
  • ½ tsp bicarbonate of soda
  • ½ tsp salt

Wet ingredients

  • 1 large egg, lightly whisked
  • 1 cup (250 mL) milk, full-fat or low-fat
  • ¼ cup (60 g) sour cream or plain yoghurt
  • ⅓ cup (85 mL) neutral vegetable oil
  • ¼ cup finely chopped fresh parsley
  • 1 garlic clove, crushed

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 180°C conventional / 160°C fan-forced.
  2. Microwave butter with 2 crushed garlic cloves in 30-second bursts until melted; stir.
  3. Generously brush a 12-hole standard muffin tin, including the upper edges, with the garlic butter. Reserve some for brushing the baked muffins.
  4. Whisk the dry ingredients together in one bowl.
  5. Whisk the wet ingredients together in another bowl.
  6. Add wet ingredients to dry and stir briefly (the original suggests about 8 folds). Add cheese and fold only until remaining flour is incorporated. Do not overmix; the batter should be thick.
  7. Fill 11 muffin holes generously, nearly to the top.
  8. Bake 22–25 minutes, until golden, crusty and springy when gently pressed in the centre.
  9. Transfer to a cooling rack, brush tops with remaining garlic butter and allow to cool slightly before serving.

Notes

  • The source describes the flavour as similar to cheesy garlic bread in muffin form.
  • Minimal mixing is important for a soft, tender texture.
  • The recipe uses 11 holes of a standard 12-hole muffin tin to achieve generously filled muffins.
  • Only the photographed first page is available; any additional source notes and variations were not captured.
