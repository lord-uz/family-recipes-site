Winter tomato soup

Status: Previously tried by the family; any personal adjustments not yet recorded.
Source: Winter Tomato Soup, photographed cookbook page (book title not visible).
Cooking time: Approximately 1 hour of simmering, plus preparation.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup stockpot soup mix (dry soup mix of pulses/grains, as sold for soups)
  • 3 cups (750 mL) beef stock, preferably homemade
  • 1 × 400 g can whole peeled tomatoes, preferably no added salt
  • 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 tsp sugar
  • Extra water, as needed during simmering

Method

  1. Rinse the stockpot soup mix thoroughly under hot running water.
  2. Put the rinsed soup mix and beef stock in a large saucepan.
  3. Roughly chop the canned tomatoes. Add the tomatoes and all their can juices, Worcestershire sauce and sugar to the saucepan.
  4. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer gently for approximately 1 hour, stirring occasionally.
  5. Add a little extra water if the soup becomes too thick before the soup mix is tender. Serve hot, optionally with crusty bread rolls.

Notes

  • Stockpot soup mix: Use a dried soup mixture intended for long simmering (often containing split peas, lentils, barley or similar ingredients); different brands have different cooking requirements. Check the mix for soaking instructions and simmer until the pulses/grains are tender.
  • The original calls for 3 cups of beef stock and one 400 g can of tomatoes; this is distinct from the smooth tomato and sweet potato soup.
  • Original print shows 753 kJ (180 cal) per serving, but does not show a clear serving count in the supplied photo.
  • Crusty rolls are a suggested accompaniment, not an ingredient in the soup.
