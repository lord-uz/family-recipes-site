Chorizo and pea slice (family-sized batch)

Status: Tested and adjusted by the family. Use the handwritten quantities below, not the smaller amounts in the original printout.
Source: Wattie's, Chorizo & Pea Slice (photographed recipe).
Yield: One large family dinner with enough leftovers for the next day (the family uses one appropriately sized baking dish).

Ingredients — family's handwritten batch

  • 2 onions, chopped
  • 300 g spicy chorizo, chopped into small pieces
  • 225 g grated cheese (original specifies low-fat)
  • 3 cups frozen baby peas
  • 12 eggs
  • 4½ cups milk (approximately 1.125 L using Australian 250 mL cups)
  • 2 cups self-raising flour
  • Small amount of oil for frying
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 160°C fan-forced. Lightly grease a large baking dish.
  2. Heat a little oil in a pan and fry the onions and diced chorizo over medium heat until the onions soften.
  3. Spread the onion and chorizo mixture in the baking dish. Scatter grated cheese and frozen peas evenly over the top.
  4. Beat eggs, milk and self-raising flour together until smooth; season with salt and pepper. Pour evenly over the filling.
  5. Bake until the centre is set and firm to the touch. The original smaller recipe specifies 50–55 minutes; the family's enlarged batch may need longer depending on the depth of their baking dish. Check the centre for doneness rather than relying only on time.
  6. Stand for 10 minutes before slicing. Serve warm, optionally with tomato relish/chutney and a green salad.

Family adjustments and practical notes

  • Handwritten changes on the printout: 2 onions; 300 g chorizo; 225 g cheese; 3 cups peas; 12 eggs; 4½ cups milk; 2 cups self-raising flour.
  • Family meal planning: These quantities are deliberately sized for dinner plus enough leftovers for the following day. The family has a single suitably sized baking dish.
  • The original suggests bacon as an alternative to chorizo.
  • Cooking duration for the larger family batch was not recorded; update after the next cook.
