Greek lemon chicken and rice soup (avgolemono)

Status: Proposed — new family dish.
Serves: Approximately two family dinners (around 7 adult-equivalent portions).
Time: Roughly 35–40 minutes active, 60–70 minutes total.
Style: A substantial chicken and rice soup with a silky lemon-and-egg finish, not a cream soup.

Ingredients

  • 900 g–1 kg boneless skinless chicken thighs (or breasts, but thighs are more forgiving)
  • 1.5 L gluten-free chicken stock, plus 500–750 mL water as needed
  • 2 medium carrots, diced
  • 2 celery stalks, diced
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 180–220 g uncooked white rice (medium-grain is ideal; long-grain is fine)
  • 3 large eggs
  • 2 lemons (juice to taste; begin with 60–75 mL)
  • 1–2 bay leaves (optional)
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Small handful fresh dill or parsley, to serve

Method

  1. In a large soup pot, soften onion, carrots and celery in olive oil over medium heat for 5–8 minutes without browning.
  2. Add stock, 500 mL water, bay leaf and chicken. Simmer gently for 15–20 minutes, until the chicken is cooked through. Lift out chicken, allow to cool briefly, then shred or dice.
  3. Add uncooked rice to the broth and simmer according to packet directions, typically 12–18 minutes, until tender. Return chicken to the pot. Add water if needed for a soup-like consistency, especially because rice continues absorbing liquid.
  4. In a large heatproof bowl, whisk eggs with lemon juice until completely mixed. Turn off the heat under the soup. Slowly whisk 2–3 ladles of hot broth into the eggs, a little at a time, to temper them.
  5. Pour the warmed egg mixture back into the soup in a thin stream, stirring constantly. Gently warm over low heat just until the broth becomes silky and slightly thickened; do not boil. Taste and adjust lemon, salt and pepper.
  6. Serve with fresh dill or parsley. Remove any bay leaves.

Leftovers and family notes

  • Allow soup to cool promptly and refrigerate in shallow containers. Reheat to steaming hot gently over low heat without boiling, stirring often, to reduce the risk of the egg mixture curdling.
  • Cooked rice absorbs broth during storage. Add a little water or stock to thin when reheating.
  • The flavour should be bright and lemony but not sharply acidic. Add lemon incrementally to suit the children.
  • Stock on hand is unspecified; reserve it for the beef casserole if appropriate and use the listed chicken stock for this soup.
