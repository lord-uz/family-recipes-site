Potato and bacon frittata

Status: Tried by the family.
Serves: About 2–3 adults; scale up for the whole family of 3.5 adult portions.
Time: Approximately 25 minutes active, 40 minutes total.

Ingredients

  • 6 eggs
  • 500 g potatoes, washed (skins may stay on)
  • 200–250 g bacon, diced
  • 60–120 g baby spinach
  • 100–150 g feta, crumbled
  • 1 small onion or a piece of leek (optional)
  • A handful of cherry tomatoes (optional)
  • A little oil, salt and black pepper
  • Grated cheddar (optional)

Method

  1. Boil the washed potatoes until just tender; drain and slice, leaving the skins on if preferred.
  2. Fry the bacon in an oven-safe frying pan; add onion or leek, if using, and cook until softened.
  3. Stir in the spinach until wilted. Arrange the sliced potatoes in the pan.
  4. Beat the eggs with a little pepper. Pour over the filling, then scatter over feta and optional tomatoes or cheddar.
  5. Cook gently on the stovetop until the bottom starts to set. Finish under the oven grill until the egg is set through; watch closely to prevent burning.
  6. Rest briefly, slice and serve.

Coeliac notes

Use bacon and other packaged ingredients verified suitable for a strict gluten-free diet. Avoid cross-contact from pans, utensils and work surfaces. Do not rely solely on a ‘may contain’ statement to assess declared gluten ingredients.

Family notes

  • The version actually cooked was potato and bacon frittata with cherry tomatoes, onion, and cheese.
