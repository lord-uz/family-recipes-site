Lentil and bacon stew

Status: Previously tried by the family; specific family adjustments not yet recorded.
Source: Lentil and bacon stew, by Krenzlich, Allrecipes UK & Ireland (from a printed recipe).
Serves: 8. Prep: 15 minutes. Cook: 40 minutes. Total: 55 minutes.

Ingredients

  • 150 g bacon, diced
  • 2 onions, chopped
  • 6 garlic cloves, minced
  • 6 carrots, sliced
  • 2 × 400 g tins diced tomatoes, including juice
  • 3 bay leaves
  • 1½ tsp dried thyme
  • 400 g dried red lentils
  • 2 tsp salt (adjust to taste, particularly if stock and bacon are salty)
  • 1.5 L chicken stock
  • 6 tsp (2 tbsp) balsamic vinegar
  • 6 tbsp chopped fresh parsley

Method

  1. In a large saucepan over medium heat, fry diced bacon for about 5 minutes until crispy. Drain off most of the rendered fat.
  2. Add onions, garlic and carrots. Stir and cook for about 2 minutes, beginning to soften the vegetables.
  3. Add tomatoes with their juice, bay leaves, thyme, red lentils, salt and chicken stock. Bring to a boil.
  4. Cover, reduce heat and simmer for approximately 30 minutes, until the lentils are soft. Stir occasionally and add water if the mixture becomes too thick.
  5. Remove bay leaves, stir in balsamic vinegar and garnish with fresh parsley.

Notes

  • Red lentils soften and partly break down, making this a thick, hearty stew.
  • The original recipe specifies 2 tsp salt; it may be sensible to start lower and adjust at the end because bacon and stock contribute salt.
  • The provided printout is truncated after “Garnish with parsley and”; parsley is the confirmed garnish.
