Basic crêpes

Status: Family-tested and works well. Source: Breville BCP200 manual, p. 12.

Ingredients

  • 225 g plain wheat flour (1½ cups)
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • 320 ml milk (1¼ cups)
  • 3 eggs (approximately 60 g each)
  • 30 g melted butter (1½ tbsp)
  • 1 tsp vanilla essence

Method

  1. Sift flour and salt together.
  2. Whisk milk, eggs, melted butter and vanilla in another bowl. Add this gradually to the flour to make a lump-free batter.
  3. Heat the Breville crêpe plate at setting 2. Ladle about ¼ cup batter onto the plate and quickly spread thinly.
  4. Cook for roughly 45–60 seconds, until the surface sets, then turn with a heat-safe non-metal spatula and finish until lightly golden.
  5. Repeat, separating cooked crêpes with baking paper if they stick. Serve with preferred fillings.

Family / coeliac notes

  • The original wheat-flour recipe has been tried and approved, not a gluten-free substitute.
  • For the coeliac family member, prepare a separate verified gluten-free recipe on thoroughly cleaned equipment, or use a dedicated cooking surface to avoid cross-contact. Gluten-free substitution has not been tested here.
  • Batter can be prepared ahead and refrigerated; stir before cooking. Morning cooking time will depend on number made.
