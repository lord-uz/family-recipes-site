Beef bolognese with pasta

Status: Proposed. Serves: Two dinners for 3.5 adult-equivalent portions.

Ingredients

  • 1.2–1.3 kg beef mince
  • 2 × 700 g bottles passata
  • 2 onions, finely diced
  • 2 carrots, finely diced or grated
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tsp dried oregano or mixed Italian herbs
  • Salt and pepper
  • For two dinners: about 500–600 g regular dry pasta and 200–250 g gluten-free dry pasta (adjust to appetites)

Method

  1. Soften onions and carrots in olive oil in a large pan, 5–7 minutes. Add garlic.
  2. Add mince in batches and brown thoroughly, breaking it up.
  3. Stir in passata and oregano. Simmer gently 20–30 minutes, stirring occasionally. Season to taste.
  4. Serve half the sauce with freshly cooked pasta. Cool the other half promptly in shallow containers, refrigerate, and reheat thoroughly the following evening.
  5. Cook gluten-free and wheat pasta in separate pots with separate colanders and utensils.

Family notes

  • Planned for Tuesday and Wednesday. Sauce is shared; pasta types are separate for coeliac safety.
  • Not yet family-tested.
