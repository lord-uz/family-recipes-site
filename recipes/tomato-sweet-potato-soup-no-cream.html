Tomato soup with potatoes (no cream)

Status: Previously tried by the family; any adjustments are not yet recorded.
Source: Katia, The Clever Meal, Tomato soup with potatoes (no cream) (photographed printout).
Serves: 3. Prep: 8 minutes. Cook: 20 minutes. Total: 28 minutes.

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp olive oil, plus extra for serving
  • 1 onion, sliced or chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 2 × 400 g cans tomatoes (preferably good-quality plum tomatoes)
  • 1 large sweet potato, about 250 g, cut into chunks
  • 480 mL vegetable stock or hot water, plus more if a thinner consistency is preferred
  • ¼–½ tsp salt, or to taste
  • ⅛ tsp black pepper, plus more for serving
  • Small handful fresh basil leaves, optional

Method

  1. Heat olive oil in a large pot over low–medium heat. Gently cook the onion and garlic for 5–6 minutes until soft and translucent, stirring occasionally.
  2. Add tomatoes, cubed sweet potato, stock or water, salt and pepper. Stir, bring to a simmer over medium heat and cook for about 20 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the sweet potato is soft.
  3. Remove from heat and cool briefly. Add basil if using and blend until smooth with a stick blender, or carefully blend in batches.
  4. Taste and adjust the seasoning or thickness with additional water/stock.
  5. Serve warm or hot, optionally with a drizzle of olive oil, freshly ground pepper and bread.

Notes

  • Although the original title says “potatoes”, the ingredient list specifies sweet potato (250 g). This entry follows the photographed ingredients.
  • The soup is creamy from blending the vegetables; no dairy cream is used.
  • The photographed recipe is described as vegan/vegetarian.
