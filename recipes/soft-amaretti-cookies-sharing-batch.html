Soft amaretti cookies — large sharing batch

Status: Previously tried by the family; handwritten sharing-batch quantities take priority over the printed original.
Category: Dessert / sweet baking, not a meal.
Source: Love & Olive Oil, Soft Amaretti Cookies (https://www.loveandoliveoil.com/2018/12/soft-amaretti-cookies.html).

Ingredients — handwritten quantities

  • 600 g almond flour or finely ground almonds, sifted
  • 600 g granulated sugar
  • 3 pinches salt (handwritten marking appears to indicate 3)
  • 7 large egg whites
  • ¾ tsp lemon juice
  • 1½ tsp almond extract
  • Icing sugar (confectioners' sugar), for rolling

Handwriting check: The markings are interpreted as 600 g almonds, 600 g sugar, 3 pinches salt, 7 egg whites, ¾ tsp lemon juice and 1½ tsp almond extract. Check egg-white count if the original note was intended differently.

Method

  1. Heat oven to 150°C conventional (300°F). Line baking trays with baking paper or silicone mats. Stacking two similar trays together helps prevent overly browned bases.
  2. Whisk almond flour, granulated sugar and salt in a large bowl.
  3. Beat egg whites and lemon juice to soft peaks.
  4. Add the beaten whites and almond extract to the dry ingredients. Mix until a soft, sticky dough forms; gentle kneading by hand is fine.
  5. Dust your hands with icing sugar. Form small balls approximately 2.5 cm across, roll in icing sugar and space about 2.5 cm apart on lined trays.
  6. Bake until the tops crack and bases are barely golden: the original says 30–35 minutes with doubled trays, or around 25 minutes using single trays. Keep an eye on the batches, as ovens and tray setups vary.
  7. For a crisper result, bake approximately 5 minutes longer, until the tops begin to colour. For a soft, marzipan-like centre, avoid overbaking.
  8. Cool briefly on the trays, then transfer to a rack. Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 5 days (original guidance).

Family notes

  • Batch enlarged for sharing as dessert, rather than serving as a main meal.
  • The printed recipe's yield of 24–28 cookies applies to the smaller original recipe, not this handwritten batch. Actual large-batch yield depends on ball size.
  • If baking multiple trays, allow sufficient room for air circulation; the bake time refers to each tray/batch, not the whole production run.
