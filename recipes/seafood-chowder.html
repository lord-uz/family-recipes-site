Gluten-free seafood chowder

Status: Tried by the family; worked well.
Serves: Approximately 3–4 adults.
Time: Approximately 25 minutes active, 40 minutes total.

Ingredients

  • 400–500 g white fish, thawed if frozen, cut into 3–4 cm pieces
  • 200–250 g raw peeled prawns
  • 3–4 medium potatoes, diced
  • 1 leek, sliced and washed
  • 1–2 celery stalks, finely sliced
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 700–750 mL gluten-free stock
  • 250 mL cream
  • Corn kernels, to taste (optional)
  • Cornflour mixed with a little cold water, if additional thickening is needed
  • Butter or olive oil
  • Black pepper, herbs and lemon to finish

Method

  1. In a large saucepan, soften the leek and celery in butter or olive oil; add garlic near the end.
  2. Add potatoes and stock. Simmer for about 12–15 minutes, until the potatoes are tender.
  3. Mash some of the potatoes directly in the pot to thicken the chowder, leaving plenty of pieces intact.
  4. Add cream and optional corn. If needed, add a small amount of cornflour slurry and simmer briefly until thickened.
  5. Add white fish and simmer gently for about 4–5 minutes, until nearly cooked.
  6. Add prawns near the end and cook until just opaque and fully cooked. Avoid boiling vigorously, which toughens seafood.
  7. Season, add herbs and finish with a squeeze of lemon.

Coeliac notes

Use stock and cornflour verified suitable for a strict gluten-free diet, and avoid cross-contact.

Family notes

  • The chowder was well received.
  • Possible future variation: include a wider selection of seafood.
