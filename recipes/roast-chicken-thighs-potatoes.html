Paprika roast chicken thighs with potatoes

Status: Proposed. Yield: Enough chicken for two dinners (about 7 adult-equivalent portions).

Ingredients

  • 1.5–1.7 kg boneless chicken thighs
  • 1.2–1.5 kg potatoes for the first dinner
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tsp sweet paprika
  • 2–3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 lemon
  • Salt and pepper
  • For salad: ½ small cabbage and 2 carrots, with lemon juice and olive oil

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 200°C. Cut potatoes into wedges and toss with some oil, salt and pepper.
  2. Coat chicken with garlic, paprika, oil, lemon juice and a little salt. Arrange on a large tray, with potatoes on a second tray if crowded.
  3. Roast approximately 35–45 minutes, turning potatoes as needed, until chicken reaches 75°C internally and potatoes are browned.
  4. Shred cabbage and carrots for a simple side salad.
  5. Serve about half the chicken with potatoes and salad. Cool the remaining chicken promptly in shallow containers and refrigerate.
  6. For the second dinner, reheat chicken thoroughly and serve with fresh rice and vegetables.

Family notes

  • Planned for Friday with leftovers on Saturday.
  • Keep the second dinner's carbohydrate component separate for flexibility.
  • Not yet family-tested.
