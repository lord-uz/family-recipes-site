Simple stovetop macaroni and cheese

Status: Tested — a deliberately simple family weekday dinner.
Serves: Two adults, a teenager and a child (about 3½ adult-equivalent servings).
Time: Around 30–35 minutes. No baking required.

Ingredients

  • 350–400 g regular dried macaroni (adjust based on how much the family wants)
  • 100–150 g gluten-free short pasta for the family member needing it (reduce regular macaroni if using the larger GF portion)
  • 350 mL milk
  • 180-200 g grated cheddar cheese, preferably a combination of tasty and mild cheddar
  • 25 g butter
  • 20–25 g cornflour (cornstarch), for thickening the cheese sauce
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Optional: ½ tsp Dijon mustard or a pinch of paprika

Simple side

  • 1 cucumber and 2–3 tomatoes, sliced, with a little salt and olive oil or lemon

Method

  1. Set two pots of salted water to boil if making regular and gluten-free pasta separately. Cook each type until just tender, following its packet timing. Keep separate utensils and drainers for the gluten-free portion.
  2. While pasta cooks, melt butter over medium-low heat in a saucepan. Whisk cornflour with a little cold milk into a smooth slurry, then add the rest of the milk and slurry to the butter, whisking.
  3. Gently heat, stirring, until the sauce starts to thicken and coat a spoon, about 3–5 minutes. Do not boil vigorously.
  4. Remove the pan from the heat. Add grated cheddar in several additions, stirring until smooth. Season with pepper, a little salt if needed, and optional mustard. Add a splash more milk if the sauce is too thick.
  5. Divide sauce between the regular and gluten-free pasta portions, using separate clean utensils. Serve promptly alongside cucumber and tomato salad.

Family notes

  • Simple stovetop version rather than a baked macaroni cheese with a crust.
  • Cheese sauce can be prepared once, then split across the two kinds of pasta.
  • Cornflour is the thickener rather than wheat flour. Check cheese, mustard and stock-style additives where relevant.
  • If reheating, loosen with a splash of milk and warm gently.
  • Almost everyone liked it.
